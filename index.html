Case study · Own product

SwiftFix — on-demand home-services marketplace

The problem. Customers struggle to find trustworthy technicians, and technicians lose income to unreliable bookings and payment disputes.

  • Matching ranks nearby technicians by distance, skills, availability and a nightly trust score, with weights that tune themselves after each rated job.
  • Escrow payments: held on booking, released on verified completion, refunded or split on dispute.
  • Trust & safety: photo and QR job verification, chat scanning that blocks off-platform deals, fraud flags, SOS alerts with live tracking.
  • Security: OTP + JWT with token revocation, TOTP MFA written from scratch, rate limiting, audit logs.

Case study · Professional work (UK restaurant ordering platform)

A merchant app that prints reliably and never loses an order

The problem. Every online order must arrive instantly, print on whatever printer the kitchen owns, and update correctly even when the Wi-Fi drops mid-service.

  • Printing engine: templates → receipt composer → ESC/POS text or raster image → driver, for 58 and 80 mm paper, with a live-preview template designer.
  • Printer discovery: Bluetooth, USB and Wi-Fi (mDNS, network scan, Epson discovery) with per-printer routing rules.
  • Reliable updates: unconfirmed actions are queued, re-checked against the server and re-sent only if still valid.

Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Hilt · Room · ~38,000 lines · ~180 tests. Built as an employee; client names, code and screens are confidential.
